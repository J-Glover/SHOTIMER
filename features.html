AV Engineering Specs

Designed for live production rigs and broadcast racks.

Shotimer integrates into existing venue audio-visual infrastructure with zero proprietary dongles or driver installations.

Stage Monitors & Floor Wedges

Output directly from any laptop or Mac Mini to HDMI distribution amps, SDI converters, or NDI encoders.

  • Auto-fitting viewport: Text scales dynamically up to 24vw, remaining legible across 40ft distances.
  • Screen Wake Lock API: Prevents confidence displays from falling asleep during long opening remarks.
  • OLED Pitch Black (#000000): Matches LED video wall letterbox boundaries without glowing light spill.

Stream Deck & Companion Integration

Give your show director tactile physical push buttons on an Elgato Stream Deck via local REST API hooks.

  • Play / Pause toggle button: Instant trigger mapped to hardware key with live color feedback.
  • +1m / -1m Bumpers: Adjust presenter remaining time on the fly without looking away from cameras.
  • Emergency Blackout Key: Instantly clear screen to black during sensitive breaks.
