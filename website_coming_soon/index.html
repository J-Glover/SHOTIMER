Dead Simple

How Shotimer works in 3 easy steps.

No cables between devices, and nothing for your speakers to download or install.

01

Point Any Screen to the Stage

Open your viewer link or scan the QR code on any TV, iPad, or laptop facing the speaker. Giant, high-contrast numerals automatically fill the screen so they can read remaining time from across the room.

02

Control from Your Pocket

Scan the controller QR code with your phone or open it on your laptop. Start, pause, or add 2 minutes with a single tap from anywhere in the venue over Wi-Fi or cellular data.

03

Keep Everyone on Schedule

The screen changes color automatically as time runs out (Green → Yellow → Red). Send silent text notes like "Wrap up" directly to the stage screen without interrupting the speaker.
