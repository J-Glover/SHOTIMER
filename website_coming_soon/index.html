Dead Simple

How Shotimer works in 3 easy steps.

No cables between devices, and nothing for your speakers to install.

1

Open on the Speaker's Screen

Open your viewer link on any TV, iPad, or laptop facing the speaker. Giant, high-contrast numbers fill the display so they can read it effortlessly from across the room.

2

Control from Your Phone

Scan the QR code with your smartphone or open the controller on your laptop. Start, pause, or add 2 minutes with a single tap from anywhere in the venue.

3

Keep Everyone on Schedule

The screen changes color automatically as time runs out (Green → Yellow → Red). Send silent text notes like "Wrap up" directly to the screen without interrupting.

Everything You Need

Built to keep presentations running smoothly.

No complicated settings or technical jargon. Just clear timing that presenters love.

Giant Speaker Display

Unmissable numbers your speaker can see from across the room.

The display automatically scales up to fill any TV screen, computer monitor, or tablet. Clean, high-contrast numerals mean your speaker always knows their remaining time at a single glance.

  • Automatic color warnings: Reassuring green → yellow warning at 2 minutes → bright red at 30 seconds.
  • Never goes to sleep: Prevents stage laptops and tablets from dimming or locking mid-presentation.
  • Zero software required: Runs directly inside Chrome, Safari, Edge, or tablet browsers.
Silent Stage Messages

Communicate with your speaker without saying a word.

Need the speaker to wrap up for lunch, switch microphones, or take an audience question? Type a quick note from your phone and it appears right on their screen.

  • No awkward hand waving: Send discreet reminders from the back of the room without disrupting their speech.
  • Quick 1-tap presets: Built-in buttons for "2 Mins Left", "Wrap Up", and "Audience Q&A".
  • Gentle or prominent: Choose between a subtle bottom banner or a noticeable strobe flash for urgent notices.

Wireless Remote Control

Control the countdown from anywhere in the room.

Sitting at the back table or walking around the venue? Open the controller on your phone or laptop. Every tap updates the stage screen instantly with zero delay.

  • Instant 1-tap buttons: Start, pause, reset, or add +1 min and +5 mins with one click.
  • Auto-advance linking: Automatically starts the next presenter's timer when the previous one finishes.
  • Rock-solid accuracy: Never freezes or loses time, even if your phone screen dims or you check an email.
